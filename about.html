About the Club

The Hardware Acceleration Club of KUET is a student-led community focused on designing high-performance computing systems and practical accelerator solutions for real-world problems.

What We Do

  • Project-based learning on FPGA, RISC-V, and embedded AI.
  • Technical workshops and research mentorship.
  • Team participation in national and international competitions.
  • Knowledge sharing through talks and peer review sessions.

Who Can Join

  • Any KUET student interested in hardware/software co-design.
  • Beginners eager to learn fundamentals.
  • Advanced members interested in publication-grade work.
